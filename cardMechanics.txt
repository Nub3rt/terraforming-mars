Cards not included:
  Cards that place special tiles
  Cards that place a city on Ganymede or Phobos (Ganymede Colony and Phobos Space Haven)
  Search for Life (reveal and discard a card, if it has a microbe tag, add a science resource here. Worth 3 VPs if you have at least one science resource here)
  Flooding (place an ocean tile, you may remove 4 M€ from a player owning a tile next to it)
  Special Design and Adaptation Technology (global parameter requirements are +/- 2)
